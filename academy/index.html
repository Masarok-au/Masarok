How it works

Every course follows the same five moves

  1. LearnA short lesson with a clear example. Tap for an Arabic explanation.
  2. PractiseInteractive exercises with instant feedback and a tip after each mistake.
  3. ProduceWrite a paragraph, record an answer or make a real decision.
  4. CheckpointA short mixed test. Pass it to finish the course.
  5. Back to your journeyYour Guide me step is ticked off, with points, badges and new outfits.

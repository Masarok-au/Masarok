Masarok Academy

AI coach ✨

Detailed feedback on your IELTS writing, IELTS speaking and application statements, marked against the official criteria, with corrected sentences and a stronger version of your own text.

What it checks

  • 📝 IELTS Writing Task 1 and Task 2An estimated band for each of the four criteria, with your most important mistakes fixed.
  • 🗣️ IELTS SpeakingFrom the transcript of your recorded answer: fluency, vocabulary and grammar. Pronunciation can't be judged from text.
  • ✍️ Application statementsUCAS, Common App, Genuine Student answers, motivation letters, statements of purpose and research proposals: clarity, examples, reflection, fit and language.

Look for “✨ AI feedback” under the free coach in any writing box, and after each recording when the transcript is on.

What feedback looks like

A sample on a short practice essay. Your own feedback is written for your text, in English or Arabic.

Good to know

  • Feedback is a practice estimate written by AI. It isn't an official score, and it can be wrong.
  • Masarok isn't connected to IELTS, the British Council, IDP, UCAS, the Common App or any government body.
  • Your text is sent to the Masarok server and to Anthropic, the AI provider, only to create your feedback. Masarok doesn't keep it. The server keeps only your code and its credits.
  • Payments are handled by Stripe. Masarok never sees your card details.
  • If feedback fails, your credit is returned automatically. For any problem with a payment, message me with your receipt.
  • If you're under 18, ask a parent before you buy.
